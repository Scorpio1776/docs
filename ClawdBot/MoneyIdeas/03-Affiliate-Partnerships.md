with minimal effort. ClawdBot is well-positioned to earn through tool
recommendations, referral programs, and strategic partnerships.

## Partnership Models

### 1. White-Label Partnerships

License ClawdBot technology to other businesses:

- **Marketing agencies**: They resell chatbots to their clients using your tech
- **Web development firms**: They bundle chatbots with website packages
- **IT service providers**: They offer AI chatbots as part of their service stack

**Revenue model:** 30-50% revenue share or flat monthly licensing fee

---

### 2. Integration Partnerships

Partner with complementary platforms:

- **Website builders** (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress)
  - Build a ClawdBot plugin/widget
  - Listed in their marketplace = free distribution
  - Revenue through premium features

- **Help desk platforms** (Freshdesk, Help Scout)
  - Build integrations that add AI to their platform
  - Co-marketing opportunities
  - Revenue share on referred customers

- **CRM platforms** (HubSpot, Pipedrive)
  - ClawdBot as a conversational layer
  - Listed in their app marketplace
  - Lead generation for both parties

---

### 3. Referral Program (Your Own)

Create a referral program where users earn by referring others to ClawdBot:

**Structure:**
- Referrer gets 20% commission on first 3 months of referred user's plan
- Referred user gets 10% off first month
- Top referrers get upgraded to higher tiers for free

**Implementation:**
- Unique referral links per user
- Dashboard to track referrals and earnings
- Monthly PayPal/Stripe payouts
- Cost: $0 upfront (you only pay when you earn)

---

### 4. Content Creator Partnerships

Partner with YouTubers, bloggers, and influencers:

- Offer free Pro accounts to creators with audiences > 1,000
- Provide custom affiliate links with higher commission (25-30%)
- Create co-branded content and tutorials
- Sponsor their content in exchange for ClawdBot mentions

**Target creators in these niches:**
- AI/tech reviewers
- Small business advice
- Developer content creators
- Productivity/automation channels

---

### 5. Agency Partner Program

Formalize relationships with agencies:

**Silver Partner (Free):**
- 10% commission on referrals
- Co-branded marketing materials
- Partner directory listing

**Gold Partner (5 referrals/quarter):**
- 20% commission
- Priority support for their clients
- Joint webinars and case studies
- Early access to new features

**Platinum Partner (15 referrals/quarter):**
- 30% commission
- Dedicated partner manager
- Custom features and integrations
- Revenue share on white-label deals

## Bootstrapping Affiliate Revenue

### Quick Wins ($0 investment)
1. Sign up for affiliate programs today (most are free and instant)
2. Add affiliate links to ClawdBot's recommendations
3. Write comparison articles (ClawdBot blog) targeting buyer-intent keywords
4. Create YouTube tutorials showing ClawdBot + partner tools
5. Share honest tool reviews on social media with affiliate links

### Medium-Term (Month 2-3)
1. Build simple integrations with partner platforms
2. Submit ClawdBot to marketplace/plugin directories
3. Launch your referral program
4. Start outreach to content creators

### Long-Term (Month 4+)
1. Formalize agency partner program
2. Negotiate custom affiliate rates with high-volume referrals
3. Explore white-label licensing deals
4. Build co-marketing campaigns with partners
