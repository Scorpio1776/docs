# ClawdBot - Additional Revenue Streams

## Overview

Beyond SaaS, services, and affiliates, here are creative ways to monetize
ClawdBot that many people overlook.

---

## 1. API Marketplace / Pay-Per-Use

Sell ClawdBot API access to developers who want to embed AI chat in their apps.

**Pricing model:**
| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | $0 | 100 API calls/month |
| Developer | $0.005/call | Pay as you go, no commitment |
| Volume | $0.002/call | 10,000+ calls/month |
| Enterprise | Custom | SLA, dedicated support |

**Why this works:**
- Developers prefer APIs over building from scratch
- Usage-based pricing means customers pay proportional to value received
- Low barrier to entry, high switching cost once integrated

## 2. Marketplace for Bot Personas / Templates

Create a marketplace where users buy and sell pre-built bot personas:

**Examples:**
- "Legal Assistant" persona - trained on legal FAQ patterns ($15)
- "Real Estate Agent Bot" - handles property inquiries ($25)
- "Restaurant Host Bot" - reservations and menu questions ($10)
- "Fitness Coach Bot" - workout and nutrition guidance ($20)
- "Tech Support Bot" - troubleshooting flows ($15)

**Revenue model:**
- You take 30% of each sale
- Creators keep 70%
- You also sell your own first-party templates

## 4. Sponsored Recommendations

Once ClawdBot has users, monetize through contextual recommendations:

- When a user asks about project management, recommend Monday.com (sponsored)
- When a user asks about email marketing, recommend ConvertKit (sponsored)
- When a user asks about hosting, recommend DigitalOcean (sponsored)

**Key rules:**
- Always disclose sponsored content
- Only recommend tools you genuinely believe in
- Label clearly as "Sponsored" or "Partner recommendation"
- Allow users to opt out of sponsored content (paid plan perk)

## 5. Data & Insights (Anonymized)

Aggregate anonymized usage data to create valuable reports:

- "Top 100 Questions Small Businesses Ask AI" - free report (lead magnet)
- "AI Chatbot Industry Benchmarks" - annual report ($49 or free with email)
- "Customer Support Trends Report" - quarterly (sponsored by enterprise tools)

**Important:** Never sell individual user data. Only aggregate, anonymized
trends. Be transparent in your privacy policy.
